About Classic Sacred Exports

Classic Sacred Exports is a Madurai,India-based exporter of premium sacred products, ritual essentials, temple supplies, devotional articles, incense, and herbal wellness powders. We supply a wide range of products in bulk, including betel leaves, mango leaves, vibuthi, kumkum, rudraksha malas, dharbai grass, ashwagandha, amla and moringa powders, and brass, bronze, and copper articles.

We focus on authenticity, quality assurance, export compliance and customer satisfaction.
